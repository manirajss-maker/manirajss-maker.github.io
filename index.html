About Maniraj

I am a Senior Geotechnical Manager with over 19 years of international experience spanning India, Malaysia, Saudi Arabia, and Bahrain, currently serving as Senior Techno-Commercial Manager / Department Manager - Proposals at Fugro-Suhaimi Ltd. in Saudi Arabia.

Holding a Master of Technology in Geotechnical Engineering from IIT Madras and a Bachelor's degree in Civil Engineering from CEG, Anna University, I bring deep expertise across onshore and offshore geotechnical investigations, ground improvement, shallow and deep foundations, retaining structures, and structural foundation design.

My career has progressed through hands-on engineering roles into leadership positions overseeing proposal management, business development, and technical operations for Saudi Arabia's mega and giga infrastructure projects, including work with NEOM, Saudi ARAMCO, Red Sea Global, and various PIF-backed developments.

A licensed Professional Engineer with the Saudi Council of Engineers and a member of the Indian Geotechnical Society, I am passionate about delivering innovative, value-engineered geotechnical solutions on complex, large-scale infrastructure projects.
